## Why choose gypsum works for bedrooms?

Bedrooms have specific needs that make gypsum the ideal material. It is lightweight, fire-resistant, easy to shape and works perfectly with hidden lighting. Standard plasterboard offers around 30 minutes of fire resistance, and fire-rated boards reach 60 minutes or more, which adds a layer of safety to a room where you sleep.

- **Acoustic insulation:** Gypsum reduces external noise for peaceful sleep, especially when the cavity is filled with mineral wool.
- **Soft lighting:** Indirect LED integration that does not disturb sleep and removes harsh overhead glare.
- **Relaxing design:** Soft shapes, recessed coves and warm colour temperatures.
- **Moisture control:** Gypsum naturally buffers small changes in ambient humidity.
- **Thermal comfort:** A boarded ceiling with insulation helps maintain an even sleeping temperature.

Because plasterboard is dry-fixed onto a metal frame, the work is clean and fast compared with traditional wet construction. There is very little dust once the frame is up, and most of a bedroom can be boarded in a single day. ## Bedroom ceiling designs that work

### Tray ceilings

The most recommended design for bedrooms. The central section rises slightly, creating the illusion of a higher ceiling. Combined with indirect LED lighting, the effect is relaxing and elegant. Tray ceilings suit rooms with at least 2.6 metres of height so the recess does not feel cramped.

### Cove lighting ceilings

Cove lighting is ideal for bedrooms because it creates soft, diffused light that promotes relaxation. LED strips are placed inside the ceiling frame, directing light upward and bouncing it off the surface. Warm white strips between 2700 and 3000 Kelvin produce the calmest atmosphere, while a dimmer lets you drop the level for the evening.

### Over-bed gypsum panel

A popular trend creates a decorative gypsum panel on the wall behind the bed that extends onto the ceiling. This element becomes the room focal point and can integrate indirect lighting and decorative niches. It also doubles as an acoustic improvement when built as a clad partition. To plan colour and finish for this feature, our article on [choosing paint colours for your home](/en/blog/choosing-paint-colors-for-your-home/) is a useful next step.

### Organic curves

Letting go of right angles with oval, wave-like or circular shapes creates a sense of calm and a spa-like feel. Gypsum bends easily when scored and dampened, so curved coves and rounded panels are entirely achievable.

## How does gypsum improve bedroom acoustics?

If your bedroom is near a road, noisy neighbours or other rooms, acoustic gypsum insulation is a valuable investment. A double-boarded partition with a mineral-wool-filled cavity can reduce transmitted noise by roughly 45 to 60 decibels depending on the configuration, which is the difference between hearing every passing car and sleeping undisturbed.

The principle is mass plus separation. Two layers of board separated by an air gap and absorptive insulation stop sound far better than a single rigid wall. The same logic applies to ceilings under a noisy upstairs neighbour, where a suspended gypsum ceiling with insulation cuts impact and airborne noise from above. According to the manufacturer reference at [Knauf](https://www.knauf.com/), system performance depends on board thickness, cavity depth and the quality of the seals around the perimeter, so professional installation matters.

## How is a bedroom gypsum ceiling installed?

The process is methodical and clean. First we set the perimeter level and fix a metal frame to the existing ceiling. Boards are then screwed to the frame, with openings cut for spotlights and LED channels. Joints are taped and filled with at least two coats of compound, each drying about 24 hours, then sanded smooth. Finally the surface is primed and painted with two coats of a quality interior paint such as Dulux, Jotun or Caparol, covering roughly 10 to 12 square metres per litre per coat.

Good preparation of the surrounding walls makes the finished room look professional rather than patched.
